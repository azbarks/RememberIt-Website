What's inside

Everything you need to stay connected

🔔

Reminders

See every overdue and upcoming reminder in one place. Swipe to dismiss, snooze, or delete. Filter by birthdays, anniversaries, or custom reminders.

👥

Contacts

Keep a rich log of interactions for each person — notes, events, family members. Set annual ticklers for birthdays and anniversaries automatically.

🧠

Remember

Group people you want to remember by name — colleagues from a conference, guests at a dinner, classmates. Add a hint so the name sticks.

🔧

Maintenance

Track service history and schedule reminders for your home, vehicles, and pets. Log costs and notes, and get notified when service is due.

How it works

Up and running in minutes

1

Add your people

Import from your contacts or add them manually. Include birthdays, anniversaries, and notes about what makes them memorable.

2

Set reminders

Birthdays and anniversaries create annual ticklers automatically. Add custom one-time or repeating reminders for anything else.

3

Stay connected

Get a daily notification when reminders are due. Snooze birthdays for next year, or dismiss custom reminders when done.

🧠

Your data, always with you

RememberIt syncs seamlessly across your iPhone, iPad, and Mac via iCloud. No account to create — just sign in with the Apple ID you already have.
